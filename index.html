How my workflow works:

🔍 Discovery & Objective Understanding: I start by understanding your brand, audience, communication goals, and campaign direction to identify the right visual approach.

🧠 Brainstorming & Visual Direction: I develop creative concepts, references, layouts, and visual systems aligned with your brand positioning and marketing objective.

🎨 Design Execution: The creation process begins. During this phase, I share progress updates and design previews to ensure we stay aligned before finalizing everything.

🚀 Final Optimization: After approval, I refine and optimize all assets for professional use across social media, marketplace platforms, presentations, websites, or campaigns.
